Bird vs. Drone Detection with YOLO

Designed and developed a two-class object detection system by fine-tuning pretrained YOLOv8s on video frames. The workflow included correcting class labels, comparing three training experiments on an NVIDIA T4 GPU, evaluating detections, and exporting models to ONNX.

I also investigated overlap across dataset splits and identified limitations in evaluation on unseen videos. Advisor: Dr. Tabatabaei.
